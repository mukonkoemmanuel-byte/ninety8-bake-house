About us

Ninety8 Bake House started in a home kitchen with one oven and a passion for baking. What began with birthday cake requests has grown into a home bakery offering a variety of sweet and savoury treats. From custom-made celebration cakes to freshly prepared pastries, including pies, samosas and sausage rolls, everything is made with care and prepared to order. We're still a home bakery, bringing homemade goodness to every occasion.
